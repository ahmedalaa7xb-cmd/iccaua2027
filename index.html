Venue

German University in Cairo, Main Campus
Main Entrance, Al Tagamoa Al Khames (Fifth Settlement), New Cairo City, Cairo, Egypt
  • 45–75 minutes by car from Cairo International Airport (CAI), via the Ring Road and the Katameya exit.
  • No metro to New Cairo. Use Uber or Careem, and book your ride back in advance.
  • Stay in New Cairo or the Fifth Settlement. Central Cairo is about 30 km away.
  • Visa: apply on the Egypt e-Visa portal, or check whether you can get one on arrival.
